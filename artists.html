Discover the Greatest Artists in History

Vincent van Gogh

Vincent van Gogh (1853–1890) was a Dutch Post-Impressionist painter known for his vivid colors and expressive brushwork. During his career, he created more than 2,000 artworks, including famous paintings such as Starry Night and Sunflowers. Although he struggled with personal challenges throughout his life, his work later became some of the most admired and influential art in the world.

Claude Monet

Claude Monet (1840–1926) was a French painter and one of the founders of the Impressionist movement. He became famous for capturing the effects of light and color in nature through loose brushstrokes and vibrant paintings. His most well-known works include the Water Lilies series and Impression, Sunrise, which inspired the name of the Impressionist movement.

Rembrandt

Rembrandt van Rijn (1606–1669) was a Dutch painter and printmaker of the Dutch Golden Age. He is widely admired for his mastery of light and shadow, as well as his detailed portraits and historical scenes. Some of his most famous works include The Night Watch and The Anatomy Lesson of Dr. Nicolaes Tulp.
